Privacy policy

Applies to the LaVieo desktop app and the LaYoBoo channels · Last updated: 19 November 2026 · Home · Terms of service

What LaVieo is

LaVieo is a private desktop application used by the LaYoBoo team to create Tamil story videos and publish them to the team's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public.

Google / YouTube data

  • LaVieo uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publish times, and read basic channel and video information (name, handle, view counts) for the channel the user signs in with.
  • Sign-in uses Google's OAuth. The access tokens are stored only on the user's own computer, encrypted with the operating system's protection (Windows DPAPI). They are never sent to us or to any third party.
  • LaVieo does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.
  • LaVieo's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • By using LaVieo's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • You can remove LaVieo's access at any time in your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also disconnect the channel inside LaVieo, which deletes the stored token from the computer.

Storage, cookies and deletion

  • LaVieo is a desktop app. It does not use cookies or tracking, and it has no server: everything (tokens, channel name, video statistics shown in the app) is kept only on the user's own computer.
  • YouTube data shown in the app (for example view counts) is refreshed when the user opens the channel page and is not kept longer than 30 days without being refreshed.
  • Deleting your data: click Disconnect on the channel in LaVieo, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting immediately deletes the stored token and the cached channel data from the computer. You can also email us and we will delete any data within 7 days.

Instagram / Meta data

LaVieo uses Meta's Instagram Graph API only to publish Reels to the team's own Instagram account. The access token is stored encrypted on the user's computer and is not shared.

Viewers of our videos

LaVieo does not collect any personal information about people who watch our videos. YouTube and Instagram handle viewing data under their own privacy policies.
